## 0. Register and narrative

**Register — The Operating Manual.** The homepage is the front cover, About is the founding ledger, Business is the manual that explains how the machine runs.

**The one question the page answers:** _How does this company actually operate — what does it run, what can it deliver, how is the corporate body assembled, how does capital flow through it, and how does a project get delivered?_

**The reader:** an institutional analyst, a corporate occupier evaluating a development partner, or an investor underwriting the platform. They are verifying capability and method, not being sold. Every section is an answer, not a claim.

**Narrative arc — five chapters, in operating order:**

1. **What operates** — the three operating divisions.
2. **What is standing capability** — the capability matrix.
3. **What is the corporate machine** — the entity map.
4. **What is the money loop** — capital deployment and the NDR InvIT relationship.
5. **What is the method** — the delivery process chain.

The page opens on a technical masthead that carries the client's Company Overview as the manual's **scope statement**, and closes on a quiet signature block. No story, no history, no dates — process, not time.

## 1. The ten architecture decisions

1. **Page narrative** — The Operating Manual: divisions → capability → machine → capital → method. Every section is operational documentation; there is no editorial storytelling section. `§0`.

2. **Information hierarchy** — Tier A (always on): the five chapters. Tier B (conditional): approved vertical photography, rendered only when it lands. Asset Portfolio is **not a section** — the client's asset list is pending ("shall share separately"), so Business routes to Portfolio and ships no empty shell. `§2`.

3. **Section sequence** — Technical Masthead → 01 Verticals → 02 Capabilities → 03 Corporate Structure → 04 Capital Deployment → 05 Execution → Closing. The masthead absorbs the Company Overview (homepage/About have their own overview moments; Business folds it into the manual's scope). `§3`.

4. **Visual identity** — A blueprint sheet: a hairline column grid **drawn across the page**; maroon as the working color; gold reserved for numerals, node marks and proof; one dark band only; orthogonal geometry exclusively (no circles anywhere). `§4`.

5. **Signature interaction** — The **sticky section index**: a numbered chapter rail that tracks scroll position and navigates the manual. Exactly one signature. Supporting behaviors (table-row hover, chain draw) are secondary. `§5`.

6. **Unique layout language** — The drawn grid + the left-rail index + full-width "division plates" and "spec plates" (hairline-framed sheets), not cards. The page is a set of ruled sheets. `§4`.

7. **Data visualization approach** — Four evidence devices, all orthogonal: the **entity map** (the page's geometric signature), the **capability matrix**, the **capital flow chain**, and the **delivery process chain**. No counters as a device; no circles; no timeline. `§6`.

8. **Transition rhythm** — Document-slow: plates reveal into the grid at section level; the two chains draw left→right once. No staggered card bursts, no parallax, no pulse. Reduced-motion = instant. `§7`.

9. **Mobile behavior** — The index becomes a horizontal scroll-snap chapter strip; plates stack; the matrix becomes stacked blocks; the entity map becomes a vertical orthogonal tree; the chains become vertical steppers; the drawn grid disappears. `§8`.

10. **Relationship to Portfolio and Investor Centre** — Business is the operating view; Portfolio is the record of places (what exists); Investor Centre is the financial statement (what is reported). Business routes to both and duplicates neither: it never lists assets, never repeats reported figures as a statement, and links the InvIT out to its own entity. `§10`.

## 4. Visual identity

### 4.1 The drawn grid (the page's layout signature)

A faint hairline column grid is drawn across the full height of each content band — vertical 1 px rules aligned to the 12-column container (1280 px, gutter 24). This is a **blueprint sheet** backdrop, the one device no other page has. Discipline:

- Rules: `hairline-light`, opacity ~0.5, positioned against the centered container.
- Drawn on desktop ≥1024 px only. Absent on the dark band, absent on mobile.
- The grid is _drawn_, never a boxed table container; content sits on it, not inside it.

### 4.2 Color discipline

- **Maroon = the working color.** Section top rules (2 px, `--color-maroon`), matrix headers, structure strokes, chain spine. Maroon, not gold, is the page's ruling line — About owns the gold top rule.
- **Gold = reserved for numerals, node marks, proof lines, and the single primary CTA.** A gold item on Business always marks a _number or a signal_, never a decorative band.
- **One dark band** — 04 Capital Deployment, flat `--color-maroon-dark`. The page's single punctuation. No maroon-to-charcoal gradient (homepage partnership-band collision).
- Ivory majority; ivory-dim only for the verticals register and the structure band.

### 4.3 Chapter marks

Every chapter header carries: a **2 px maroon rule**, a **gold-outlined diamond** (the logo motif, 10 px), and a **large gold Fraunces numeral** (64 px) as a left callout — the drawing-callout numbering of an engineering sheet. The diamond is system-wide geometry (Design Direction §3.2); the _circle is not used anywhere on this page_ — the lifecycle circle is About's exclusive, the capital cycle circle is the homepage's.

### 4.4 Typography roles

- **Fraunces = voice.** Masthead headline, chapter numerals, division names.
- **Inter = spec.** Body, labels, entity names (exact legal form), matrix cells, chain nodes, captions. Technical text is sans; the serif is the identity.
- **Tabular Inter** for any aligned figures (capability evidence, chain indices).
- Sentence-case throughout; small-caps only for labels and eyebrows.

### 4.5 Imagery rule

No full-bleed imagery; no imagery in the masthead; no hero scene. Approved photography enters only as hairline-framed 16:10 technical plates inside division plates (Tier B). The page is a document.

### 4.6 Density

Medium-high, tabular — the most ruled of the editorial pages, but not financial: rows are one-idea cells, not dense ledgers. Whitespace lives inside the plates, not between marketing bands.

## 5. Signature interaction — the sticky section index

- **Desktop ≥1400 px:** a fixed vertical rail at the left viewport edge (56 px wide, centered vertically, beneath the header). Renders five chapters as gold diamond + Fraunces numeral; the active chapter is marked by a maroon rule and the label is revealed (text expands on hover/active). `aria-current` tracks the section in view.
- **768–1399 px:** the rail becomes a slim horizontal chapter strip, sticky beneath the header — five links, scrollable, 48 px targets.
- **<768 px:** horizontal scroll-snap chapter strip; native links; 48 px targets.
- Fully keyboard-accessible; the index is the manual's table of contents and the page's only signature interaction.

Supporting (secondary, not signatures): capability-matrix row hover; chain draw left→right; division-plate hover (numeral to gold-light). No pulse, no parallax, no count-up — count-up is the homepage's signature.

---

## 6. Data visualization language

Four devices, all **orthogonal** (straight lines, right angles, diamonds — the opposite of About's circle and the homepage's cycle):

1. **Entity map** (§03) — the page's geometric signature. Header node → orthogonal elbow connectors → branch plates. Deck line-art language per Design Direction §3.13.
2. **Capability matrix** (§02) — a ruled coordinate grid: capability × phase, with an evidence column.
3. **Capital flow chain** (§04) — a linear left→right chain: Develop → Offer under ROFO → Monetize → Recycle → Develop. Orthogonal nodes on a maroon spine.
4. **Delivery chain** (§05) — a linear left→right process chain: Originate → Design → Procure → Build → Assure & Deliver → Operate.

Numbers appear only inside proof lines, evidence cells, and source footnotes — **one stat, one source**, per the global rule. No chart, no counter band, no metric hero.

---

## 7. Transition rhythm

Document-slow and section-level only:

- Each chapter reveals as a single plate entering the drawn grid (fade + 20 px rise, 800 ms, no stagger between children).
- The two chains draw left→right once in view (stroke draw, 900 ms; gold node diamonds pop at 50 ms stagger within the chain only).
- Matrix rows do not stagger.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: everything visible instantly; chains static; captions always visible.

---

## 8. Mobile behavior

- Masthead: document-control strip stacks above the headline; scope full-width.
- Sticky index: horizontal scroll-snap chapter strip (§5).
- Division plates: stack full-width; capability spec rows collapse beneath the write-up.
- Capability matrix: each row becomes a stacked block (phase tag, capability, scope, evidence).
- Entity map: vertical orthogonal tree — parent node top, branches stacked, connectors turn 90°.
- Capital and delivery chains: vertical steppers — gold diamonds on a left maroon rule.
- Drawn grid: hidden below 1024 px; no horizontal overflow anywhere.

## 13. Collision and freeze audit

**Business must NOT adopt (freeze lists + task guardrails):**

- Homepage exclusives: full-bleed cinematic hero · audience routing strip · client logo grid · homepage section sequence · count-up as signature · marquee · circular capital cycle.
- About exclusives: typographic ledger hero · gold top rules · ledger plates · gold-diamond lifecycle circle · board-register Leadership · 1954 founding-archive narrative · draw-in as signature.
- Investor Centre posture: no "as on" datestamps, no archive patterns, no financial-statement density.
- **No years, no dates, no historical timeline anywhere** (process, not time).
- **No circles anywhere** (orthogonal only).
- No generic card grid; no marketing feature section; no embedded EnquiryForm; no stat band; no gradient band.

**Deviations from the Page Identity Guide §2.2, justified:**

1. _Guide: "BusinessHighlights vertical cards as the three operating verticals."_ → **Division plates, not cards.** The homepage already renders the three deep-maroon cards; repeating them would collide. Same sanctioned content, recomposed as spec sheets.
2. _Guide: "ruled service tables."_ → **Capability matrix with phase semantics.** Keeps the guide's service-table intent while reserving the financial ruled table for Investor Centre.
3. _Guide index: four chapters._ → **Five chapters**, adding Corporate Structure as a numbered chapter. The entity map is approved Business scope and the page's geometric signature; it earns a chapter.
4. _Homepage partnership band: maroon-to-charcoal._ → **Flat dark maroon.** Avoids the gradient-band collision while keeping the dark punctuation.
